# NABATI ORIGINALS — Design System

**Strict. Consistent. No deviations unless explicitly instructed.**

---

## Brand & Positioning

- **Brand:** NABATI ORIGINALS  
- **Category:** Luxury niche perfume house  
- **Specialty:** Signature fragrances · Bespoke / custom perfume creation · Private labeling & customization  
- **Personality:** Calm, confident, sensory, exclusive, artisan-led (not mass-market)

---

## Color System (Strict)

| Role | Token | Usage |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Primary** | `--color-sand` (deep sand brown / desert umber) | Surfaces, warmth |
| **Primary** | `--color-smoke` (smoked black) | Backgrounds, depth |
| **Secondary** | `--color-camel` (warm camel) | Mid-tones, imagery support |
| **Secondary** | `--color-amber` (muted amber) | Labels, supporting text |
| **Accent** | `--color-gold` (soft brushed gold) | Sparingly: highlights, links hover, borders |
| **Accent** | `--color-ivory` (off-white) | Contrast only; never full-page white |

**Rules:** No bright colors. No pure white backgrounds. No loud gradients. Contrast must feel soft, not sharp.

---

## Typography

| Level | Use | Style |
|-------|-----|--------|
| **H1** | Hero, page title | Editorial serif, large, confident spacing |
| **H2** | Section titles | Editorial serif, clear hierarchy |
| **H3** | Subsections, card titles | Editorial serif, supporting |
| **Body** | All body copy | Clean sans-serif, smaller, calm, readable |

**Rules:** Clear hierarchy H1 > H2 > H3 > body. No equal-weight text blocks. Headings lead; body supports. Never stretched or overly spaced body text.

---

## Layout & Spacing

- **Style:** Editorial, structured (not rigid), repetition with variation.
- **Spacing:** Generous but controlled. No large empty areas without purpose. Every section must feel complete.
- **No:** Template-like grids, random centering, over-minimalism.

---

## Imagery

- **Style:** Studio-lit, cinematic, high contrast, editorial (not catalog).
- **Use:** Support clarity and desire. Never flood sections. Balance text and visuals.

---

## Motion & Interaction

- **Style:** Subtle, slow, purposeful.
- **Rules:** No flashy animations or gimmicks. Hover states: premium and restrained.

---

## E-Commerce

- **Feel:** Calm, private, confident.
- **CTAs:** Visible but never loud.
- **Prices:** Clear, not hidden.
- **Add to Cart:** Obvious, not aggressive.

---

## Bespoke / Customization

- **Language:** “Bespoke Creation,” “Private Formulation,” “Crafted for You.”
- **Focus:** Process, craftsmanship, dialogue. Avoid step-by-step factory language.
- **Feel:** Personal, consultative, premium, invitation-only. Exclusive, not form-service.

---

## Global Rule

If any component feels generic, template-driven, over-designed, or under-designed, refine it until it feels intentional, complete, and premium. The site should feel designed by an experienced luxury brand team.
