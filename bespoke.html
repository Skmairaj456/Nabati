NABATI ORIGINALS offers bespoke perfume creation and private labeling for those who seek a fragrance that exists only for them—or for their brand. This is not a configurator. It is a dialogue with our perfumers, built on rare ingredients, intention, and time.

How we work

We begin with a conversation: your references, the mood you want to capture, the occasions and the skin. From there we propose a direction and a palette of materials. Each formulation is developed in-house, in small batches. Private labeling and customization for brands follow the same discipline—craft, not scale.

Timelines and investment are discussed privately. We do not publish fixed packages; every project is singular.
